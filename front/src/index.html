itren

Honest next train while you wait.

Board blank. App said on time. You waited anyway. itren is for that Rodalies platform moment: next real train, honest delay, wait or leave.

Pick origin and destination, then show next trains. Catalan, Spanish, English. Free. Private project by Ralf Roeber.

Data: Renfe Operadora (CC BY 4.0), full Cercanías / Rodalies GTFS. Direct trains only. FAQ on the site. Open source on GitHub.
